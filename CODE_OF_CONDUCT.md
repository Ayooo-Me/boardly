# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in Boardly welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on the issue, not the person.
- Assume good intent while clarifying disagreements.
- Accept feedback and prioritize the project’s users.
- Protect private information and security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized conduct, spam, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when this code is violated. Report concerns privately through the security/contact channels listed in the repository.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces and public interactions representing the project.
